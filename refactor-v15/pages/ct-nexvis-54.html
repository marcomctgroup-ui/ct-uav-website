01 / APPLICATIONS

Product introduction

CT NEXVIS is a surveillance and security UAV developed by CT UAV - a member of CT Group Hi-Tech - for patrol, monitoring, and security protection missions across border areas, industrial zones, critical infrastructure, and other high-security locations. With aerial monitoring, suspicious-target detection and tracking, abnormal-activity identification, and real-time data transmission, CT NEXVIS helps security forces proactively detect risks, issue early warnings, and respond promptly, thereby improving management, monitoring, and overall safety and security.

01

Automated patrol and surveillance

Conduct automated patrol and surveillance missions across borders, upstream forests, industrial zones, critical infrastructure, and other high-security areas. Supports scheduled flights on predefined routes and time slots.

02

Suspicious-target detection and tracking

Detect movement in low-light or nighttime conditions; identify, track, geofence, and continuously monitor suspicious targets to support security responses.

03

Intrusion detection and security alerts

Automatically detect intrusions, fence breaches, unauthorized access to restricted areas, and other unusual activities. Sends live alerts and visual feeds to the control center for timely response.

04

Traffic and area security monitoring

Monitor traffic conditions, detect illegal parking, observe crowded areas, and support the identification of potential public-security risks.

05

Remote warning and deterrence

Integrates loudspeakers, sirens, searchlights, and warning devices to deter, guide, or instruct offenders to stop violations without direct physical contact.

06

Real-time data capture and monitoring

Capture visual data, mark suspicious locations, and transmit live information to the control center for situational awareness, data archiving, and rapid decision-making.
