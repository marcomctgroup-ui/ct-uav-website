CT UAV inaugurates UAV joint venture assembly plant in the United States

CT UAV (a member of CT Group Hi-tech), together with partner TJ Aerospace, has officially inaugurated a UAV joint venture assembly plant in Garden Grove, California, marking a historic milestone for Vietnam’s UAV industry.

A UAV assembly plant in California

CT UAV and TJ Aerospace opened their joint venture UAV assembly plant in Garden Grove, California. The facility combines UAV development with aerospace manufacturing experience.

The opening marks a step in the company’s international manufacturing development.
