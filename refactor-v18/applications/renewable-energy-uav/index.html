CAPABILITY TO

Master 6 core technologies

CT UAV has defined its core strategy as fully mastering the value chain and technologies in the UAV industry. This capability is demonstrated through the control of six key technology groups

About our technology →

  • CT UAV has fully mastered the design and production of the most critical electronic components for UAVs. A highlight is the successful development of the CT-Nexus flight controller, which applies the proprietary CT-NonLinear algorithm, delivering superior stability and anti-interference performance compared to traditional PID controllers.

  • CT UAV possesses strong capabilities in developing AI Edge Systems and computer vision technologies for autonomous applications. In particular, CT UAV provides a comprehensive software suite, ranging from the Ground Control Station (GCS) and Fleet Management System to the Data Analytics Platform and the multi-layered Digital Twin Platform DT15.

  • CT UAV is self-reliant in developing autopilot systems and advanced navigation algorithms, ensuring safe and stable UAV operations.

  • CT UAV has the capability to independently research and manufacture UAV airframes and propellers using advanced composite materials.

  • Currently under development are intelligent Battery Management Systems (BMS) designed to optimize flight performance.

  • CT UAV focused on developing encrypted, anti-jamming datalink systems, with prototypes already tested at ranges exceeding 100 km, ready to replace imported solutions.
